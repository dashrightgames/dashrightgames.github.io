Learn it. Then beat it.

Rusty held the title for forty years, and every room of his dojo was built to keep it that way. You are Verdi, a rookie ninja with a brand-new forehead plate. He is the old champion with a rusted one, and he talks. A lot. Usually about how you just died.

Tight and fast

  • 96 rooms across 8 worlds
  • Wall jumps and a mid-air dash
  • Nothing moves until you do
  • Every world ends in a chase

Die, learn, retry

  • Back in the room in under a second
  • Death marks show where you fell
  • A ghost replay of all your attempts after each clear

Beat Rusty's time

  • Rusty's own record on every room
  • Finish faster and he gives up a little of his pride
  • A speedrun mode for whole worlds

Secrets off the safe path

  • 84 scrolls to collect, some in plain sight, some far off the safe path
  • Outfits at the tailor, each with its own effect
  • A secret world under the lake
  • Ferro, Rusty's old student, will show you the way

Eight worlds, eight sets of rules

Each world is built around one clear idea, then twists it until it bites.

Made for a controller

Full Xbox and PlayStation controller support with the right button prompts, and fine on a keyboard. Change any control in Settings.

Original synthesized music for every world, from a quiet forest at night to a full taiko battle in the dojo.
